## Display order

As requested, company logos are arranged roughly by valuation as checked on October 18, 2026. Public companies use market capitalization; acquired brands use historical transaction size and private companies use disclosed valuations where available. Smaller companies without comparable current valuations follow in an approximate editorial order. Universities and research institutes follow the companies and are not ranked by valuation. Apple is placed at the far right of the first desktop row at the site owner’s request. No valuation figures or ranking claims appear on the website.
