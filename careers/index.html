Computational Simulation Engineer

We are seeking a Computational Simulation Engineer to join our team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in computational electromagnetics, multiphysics modeling, and device simulation, with expertise in methods such as FDTD (Finite-Difference Time-Domain), FEM (Finite Element Method), TCAD (Technology Computer-Aided Design), and other advanced simulation techniques. Experience with Python and relevant scientific computing libraries is essential.

Responsibilities:

  • Develop, optimize, and maintain simulation algorithms across multiple computational methods (FDTD, FEM, TCAD, Monte Carlo, etc.).
  • Implement multiphysics simulations combining electromagnetic, thermal, mechanical, and electronic effects.
  • Analyze simulation results and collaborate closely with researchers and engineers on photonic, quantum, and analog device modeling.
  • Optimize simulation performance and accuracy for various device geometries and material systems.
  • Contribute to publications, technical reports, and open-source simulation frameworks.
  • Develop new simulation methodologies for emerging photonic, quantum, and analog devices.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Strong background in computational electromagnetics and numerical methods (FDTD, FEM, BEM, etc.).
  • Experience with TCAD simulation tools and semiconductor device physics.
  • Knowledge of multiphysics modeling and coupled simulation approaches.
  • Proficiency in Python and relevant libraries (NumPy, SciPy, FEniCS, MEEP, etc.).
  • Experience with parallel computing, GPU acceleration, and high-performance computing.
  • Familiarity with commercial simulation tools (COMSOL, Ansys, Silvaco, Synopsys, etc.).
  • Prior contributions to open-source simulation projects are a strong plus.
  • Experience with photonic, quantum, or analog device modeling.
  • Background in machine learning for simulation acceleration and optimization.

This is a remote position. We welcome applicants who are passionate about advancing computational methods in electromagnetics, multiphysics simulation, and photonic, quantum, and analog device modeling.
